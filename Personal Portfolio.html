About

I'm passionate about the future and human behavior. Using numbers to get closer to both.

I want my work to focus on how people will move, act, and decide next. This includes how AI reshapes daily choices, how business strategy responds to shifting demand, and how everyday behavior leaves patterns that data can pick up before the headlines do.
